iPhone alarm setup
  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Park LA from your home screen.
  4. After you park, tap Add to Calendar. That alarm rings even if notifications are off.
  5. Tap Allow iPhone alerts and choose Allow. Needs iOS 16.4 or newer, and the home-screen app.

The beep works only while this page is open and the screen is on. A locked iPhone needs the calendar alarm or a notification.
